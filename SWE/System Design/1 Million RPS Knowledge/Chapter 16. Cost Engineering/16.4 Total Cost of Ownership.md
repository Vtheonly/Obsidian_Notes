# Total Cost of Ownership

#cost/tco #cost/cloud-pricing #fundamentals

## Definition

**Total Cost of Ownership (TCO)** encompasses every cost associated with running a system — not just the obvious infrastructure charges, but the hidden, indirect, and ongoing costs that accumulate over time. Failing to account for TCO is one of the most common and most expensive mistakes in cloud architecture.

The video's $15,000/month figure for 1M RPS is the *infrastructure-only* cost. The real TCO is significantly higher when you include all components.

## TCO Components

### 1. Compute Costs
The most visible line item. In the video's architecture:
- 2× c8i.32xlarge: $12/hour
- These run 24/7, so compute alone is $8,640/month (On-Demand)

### 2. Database Costs
- 1× db.r5.16xlarge: $6/hour = $4,320/month (On-Demand)
- Includes the managed service premium (AWS operates, patches, backs up)

### 3. Storage Costs
- EBS gp3 volumes: ~$0.08/GB/month
- Additional provisioned IOPS: ~$0.065 per IOPS/month (the video provisioned 12,000 IOPS = ~$780/month)
- Storage + IOPS: ~$1.50/hour = ~$1,080/month

### 4. Network / Data Transfer Costs
**This is the most commonly overlooked cost.** AWS charges for:
- **Data transfer out** (egress): $0.09/GB for the first 10 TB/month. At 1M RPS with 6 KB responses: 6 GB/s × 86,400 s/day × 30 days = ~15.5 PB/month. Even at reduced tiers, this is **enormous** — potentially $100,000+/month at full egress pricing.
- **Load balancer hours**: ALB at $0.0225/hour × 2 = ~$33/month (minor)
- **NAT Gateway hours**: $0.045/hour + $0.045/GB processed
- **Cross-AZ data transfer**: $0.01/GB in each direction

> **Critical Insight:** For bandwidth-heavy workloads at 1M RPS, data transfer costs can exceed compute costs by an order of magnitude. This is why CDNs ([[14.2 Geographic Distribution]]) are essential — they absorb traffic at the edge, dramatically reducing origin egress costs.

### 5. Monitoring and Observability
- CloudWatch metrics, logs, and dashboards
- Third-party tools: Datadog, New Relic, Grafana Cloud
- Custom alerting infrastructure
- Typical: $500–5,000/month depending on tooling

### 6. DNS and CDN
- Route 53 queries: $0.40/million queries (inexpensive)
- CloudFront: $0.085/GB + $0.0075/10,000 requests (US pricing)
- For a CDN-absorbing 80% of traffic, this can be $5,000–20,000/month

## Personnel: The Biggest Hidden Cost

Infrastructure costs are transparent and quantifiable. **Personnel costs are the iceberg beneath the surface.** To build, operate, and maintain a 1M RPS system, you need:

- **Backend engineers** to design, implement, and optimize the system ($150K–$300K/year each)
- **DevOps/SRE engineers** to manage infrastructure, CI/CD, monitoring ($150K–$300K/year each)
- **Database administrators** for query optimization, schema design, backup management ($140K–$250K/year)
- **Security engineers** for compliance, penetration testing, incident response ($160K–$300K/year)

A team of 5–10 engineers at an average fully-loaded cost of $250K/year = **$1.25M–$2.5M/year** ($100K–$210K/month). This often exceeds the infrastructure cost itself.

## Why Cost Engineering Is a Senior Engineer Skill

Understanding TCO is what separates senior engineers from mid-level engineers. A mid-level engineer might say "use serverless, it scales automatically." A senior engineer asks: "At our projected traffic of 500K RPS sustained, what is the 3-year TCO of serverless vs. dedicated, including data transfer, personnel, and opportunity cost?"

This analysis requires understanding [[16.1 Cloud Cost Models]], [[16.2 Reserved Instances vs On-Demand]], [[16.3 Serverless vs Dedicated]], and the full architecture in [[17.6 Real-World Architecture for 1M RPS]].

## Common Mistakes

- **Quoting only compute cost** — ignoring data transfer, storage, and monitoring
- **Not forecasting data transfer** — egress costs scale linearly with traffic and payload size
- **Ignoring personnel cost** — the team to build and run the system is often the largest expense
- **Not accounting for growth** — TCO at 100K RPS is vastly different from TCO at 1M RPS
