Advanced binary search: **Binary Search on Answer**, **Parametric Search**, and **Continuous Binary Search**.

## 2. Why It Matters

Binary search on the answer is a powerful technique for optimization problems. It transforms 'find the minimum X such that...' into a binary search over feasible values.
