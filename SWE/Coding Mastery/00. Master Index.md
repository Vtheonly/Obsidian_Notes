## 7. How to Navigate

- Open the **Graph View** in Obsidian to see how notes connect.
- Use the **tag pane** to filter by topic (e.g. `#dp-knapsack`, `#graph-bfs`).
- Use **Ctrl+P → "Open Quick Switcher"** to jump to any note by name.
- Every note ends with a **Related Problems** or **Practice Problems** section linking to related notes.
