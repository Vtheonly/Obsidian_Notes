##  How to Use These Quizzes

1. **Read the chapter first.** Quizzes test comprehension, not psychic ability.
2. **Answer every question before checking answers.** Write your answer down or say it out loud.
3. **Click the callout to reveal the answer.** Self-grade honestly.
4. **If you got it wrong, re-read the linked topic note** (each quiz has a `sources:` frontmatter list).
5. **Re-take the quiz 24 hours later.** Spaced repetition locks it in.

---

##  Scoring

- **< 70%:** You need more study. Re-read the chapter, then re-take.
- **70–85%:** Decent. Review the questions you missed.
- **85–95%:** Strong. You're ready to move on.
- **> 95%:** Excellent. Try teaching the topic to someone else — that's the real test.
