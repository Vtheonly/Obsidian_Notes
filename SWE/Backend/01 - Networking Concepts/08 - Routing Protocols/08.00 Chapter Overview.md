#  Chapter 08 — Routing Protocols

> [!abstract] Chapter Goal
> Move from manual to automatic routing. Cover the three families — **distance-vector** (RIP), **link-state** (OSPF), and **path-vector** (BGP) — including their algorithms, loop-prevention mechanisms, packet types, and the famous Dijkstra shortest-path walkthrough. By the end you'll know which protocol to use where and why.

##  Learning Outcomes

After this chapter you should be able to:

- Distinguish distance-vector, link-state, and path-vector algorithms and give an example protocol for each.
- Configure RIPv2 on Cisco IOS with no auto-summary and passive interfaces.
- Explain Split Horizon, Poison Reverse, and Hold-Down timers and why each exists.
- Given a RIP update, predict whether each route will be installed, replaced, or discarded.
- Explain the OSPF cost formula and why gigabit links need `auto-cost reference-bandwidth`.
- Identify OSPF router types (IR, BR, ABR, ASBR) given a topology.
- Walk through Dijkstra's algorithm by hand from any starting node.
- Describe the BGP 4-step message exchange and how AS_PATH prevents loops.
