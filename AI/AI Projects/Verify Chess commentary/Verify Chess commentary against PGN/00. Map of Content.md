## The Big Picture

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    A[Markdown note with PGN and prose] --> B[Extract PGN / SAN / FEN]
    B --> C[Replay game with python-chess]
    C --> D[Generate FEN after every ply]
    D --> E[Run Stockfish on every position]
    E --> F[Structured facts database]

    A --> G[Extract natural language claims via LLM]
    G --> H[Symbolic verifier]
    F --> H
    H --> I[Verification report]
```

This single picture captures the entire pipeline. Every chapter in this vault is, in one way or another, an expansion of one of those boxes.
