# Product and Feature Specification

This note defines the product surface of the AI Boxing Coach. It is intentionally separate from the technical architecture report.

## Core Product

The application is a real-time AI boxing coach using one phone camera.

The coach should determine:

what was expected → what happened → how it differed → what was correct → what should be corrected.

## Training Modes

### Camera Setup

Before a session:

- full body visible
- head visible
- feet visible
- boxer centered
- appropriate distance
- adequate lighting
- one primary person

### Stance Coach

Supports:

- orthodox
- southpaw
- custom stance

Evaluates feet, knees, hips, torso, head, and guard.

### Punch Coach

Initial sequence:

1. Jab
2. Cross
3. Lead hook
4. Rear hook
5. Lead uppercut
6. Rear uppercut

Each punch is evaluated by phase rather than only classified.

### Defense and Footwork

Future movement classes:

- high guard
- slips
- rolls
- pull-back
- step back
- lateral movement
- pivot
- forward/backward movement

### Combination and Routine Mode

Predefined or custom sequences such as:

Jab → Cross

Jab → Cross → Lead Hook

Jab → Slip → Cross → Hook

The system must detect correct order, wrong movement, missing movement, extra movement, and timing deviations.

### Reaction Training

The coach issues a command through audio or visual prompts and measures movement correctness and response latency.

### Free Shadowboxing

No predetermined routine. The engine continuously detects learned actions and produces a post-session breakdown.

### Drill Creator

Users can define:

- duration
- sequence
- repetition count
- speed
- randomized ordering
- reaction mode
- endurance mode

### Session Review

A session should report:

- movement counts
- technique consistency
- timing
- sequence accuracy
- guard discipline
- stance stability
- footwork
- recovery quality
- recurring errors
- improving errors

## Feedback Model

Three main feedback classes:

### Positive

Examples:

- Good guard recovery
- Good hip rotation
- Good stance stability

### Corrective

Examples:

- Rear hand dropped during jab
- Lead elbow drifting outward
- Recovery too slow
- Stance became too narrow

### Sequence

Examples:

- Wrong movement
- Expected lead hook
- Movement arrived too early
- Movement arrived too late

The system should prefer specific, actionable language.

## Product Constraints

The application should not claim that:

- pose alone measures actual punching force
- estimated wrist speed is equivalent to laboratory impact measurement
- shadowboxing guarantees contact detection

Optional bag/target mode can use additional signals such as target displacement or audio cues.

## Expansion Order

Jab/stance
→ cross/hooks
→ defense/footwork
→ combinations
→ reaction drills
→ free shadowboxing
→ adaptive training
