# Boxing and Motion Glossary

## Boxing Terms

**Orthodox** — stance with the left side generally forward.

**Southpaw** — stance with the right side generally forward.

**Guard** — defensive hand and body position used to protect vulnerable targets and prepare movement.

**Jab** — typically the lead straight punch.

**Cross** — typically the rear straight punch.

**Hook** — curved punch delivered with a bent arm trajectory.

**Uppercut** — upward-directed punch.

**Slip** — evasive head movement to either side.

**Roll** — defensive movement in which the boxer changes level and moves under a punch trajectory.

**Pivot** — rotational footwork that changes body orientation while maintaining a functional base.

## Motion Terms

**Keypoint** — detected anatomical landmark such as wrist, elbow, shoulder, hip, knee, or ankle.

**Skeleton** — graph of anatomical keypoints and their relationships.

**Kinematics** — description of motion through position, velocity, and acceleration without directly modeling forces.

**Kinetics** — study of forces and moments producing motion.

**Pose normalization** — transforming raw coordinates into a representation less dependent on camera position, scale, and orientation.

**One Euro Filter** — adaptive low-pass filtering method that increases responsiveness when the signal moves quickly.

**DTW** — Dynamic Time Warping, a sequence-alignment algorithm that allows non-linear temporal correspondence.

**ST-GCN** — Spatial-Temporal Graph Convolutional Network for learning structured motion from skeleton graphs.

**Transformer** — attention-based sequence model used here for temporal motion understanding.

**Quantization** — representation of model values with lower-precision numerical formats such as INT8.

**Edge inference** — running model inference locally on the target phone rather than sending every camera frame to a server.

**Reference distribution** — statistical model of multiple technically meaningful movement examples rather than one rigid template.
