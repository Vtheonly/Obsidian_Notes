# Synthetic Generators

> Tools for generating synthetic building / 3D data.

## Procedural

- **CityEngine**: CGA shape grammar for urban generation.
- **Esri CityEngine**: commercial.
- **Building generator** (Python / Blender): procedural buildings.
- **Pylos**: parametric floor plan generator.

## Neural

- **HouseGAN**: layout generation.
- **Diffusion layout**: diffusion-based layout.
- **BuildingDiffusion**: 3D building diffusion.

## Hybrid

- **BlenderProc**: procedural + neural rendering.
- **Habitat-Sim**: simulation environment for embodied AI.

## What They Produce

- Floor plans (2D).
- Building models (3D).
- Rendered images (RGB).
- Depth maps.
- Semantic segmentation masks.
- BIM models (IFC).

See [[Synthetic RGB Depth BIM]], [[Real Datasets]], [[Domain Randomization]].
