# 08. Research Papers MOC

Every paper note follows the [[Paper Analysis Template]] without exception. The template forces every reader to ask not only "what architecture does it use?" but also "what structure does it represent, how is it discovered, how is it learned, where does it enter, is generation constrained, are invalid structures possible, how are errors detected, localized, and repaired, what is actually guaranteed, and what is merely a statistical pattern?"
