# Layout Constraints

> **Definition.** Constraints on the overall layout: room sizes, room counts, circulation patterns.

## Examples

- At least 1 bathroom per dwelling.
- At least 1 kitchen per dwelling.
- Corridor connects all rooms.
- Travel distance to exit ≤ 30 m.
- Corridor width ≥ 1.2 m.
- Bedroom area ≥ 9 m².

## Enforcement

- Compute layout metrics (room counts, areas, travel distances).
- Check against constraints.

## In Generation

Layout constraints are typically:

- Hard (code compliance).
- Validated after generation.

See [[Spatial Constraints]], [[Spatial Validation]], [[BIM Validation]].
