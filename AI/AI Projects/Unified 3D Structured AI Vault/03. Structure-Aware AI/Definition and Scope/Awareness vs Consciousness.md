# Awareness vs Consciousness

> "Aware" in AI papers is a **technical** term. It does **not** imply consciousness, intentionality, or understanding in the human sense.

It means: the model's architecture, objective, or decoding process **incorporates information about X**.

So:

- "structure-aware" = incorporates information about structure,
- "tree-aware" = incorporates information about tree relationships,
- "3D-aware" = incorporates 3D geometric information,
- "semantic-aware" = incorporates semantic information.

## Practical Translation

When a paper says "our model is X-aware", mentally translate to:

> The model's architecture explicitly uses information about X.

Then ask **how** — see [[How Structure Enters Models]].
