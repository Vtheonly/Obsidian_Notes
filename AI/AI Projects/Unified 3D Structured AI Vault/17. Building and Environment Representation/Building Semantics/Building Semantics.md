# Building Semantics

> The semantic content of a building: element types, room types, attributes, roles.

## Element Types

- Walls (load-bearing, partition, facade).
- Slabs (floor, roof, landing).
- Columns, beams (structural).
- Doors (entry, interior, fire-rated).
- Windows (fixed, operable, skylight).
- Stairs, ramps (circulation).
- Roofs (flat, pitched).
- Furniture, fixtures, equipment.

## Room Types

- Bedroom, bathroom, kitchen, living room.
- Corridor, hallway, lobby.
- Office, meeting room, server room.
- Mechanical room, electrical room.

## Attributes

- Fire rating (e.g. F90).
- U-value (thermal transmittance).
- Acoustic rating (STC).
- Material.
- Load-bearing flag.
- Manufacturer, model.

## Roles

- Structural role (load-bearing vs non-load-bearing).
- Thermal role (insulating vs conductive).
- Acoustic role (absorbing vs reflecting).
- Visual role (transparent vs opaque).

## In AI

Building semantics are node attributes in the BIM graph / tree. A generator must predict them; a validator must check them.

See [[Semantic Validation]], [[Semantic Constraints]], [[BIM Fundamentals]].
