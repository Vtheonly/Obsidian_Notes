# 2.2.3. Receptor Multi-Conformer Ensemble Conditioning and Induced-Fit Dynamics

Protein receptors populate conformational ensembles rather than a single immutable structure. SynPath-3D represents the local pocket with \(K=3\)–\(5\) correlated conformations.

\[
\mathcal P=\{P_1,\dots,P_K\}.
\]

The ensemble exposes both consensus geometry and conformational variance:

\[
\bar x_i=\frac1K\sum_kx_i^{(k)},
\qquad
\mathrm{RMSF}_i=
\sqrt{\frac1K\sum_k\|x_i^{(k)}-\bar x_i\|^2}.
\]

The production receptor representation does not substitute independent Gaussian perturbations of sidechain dihedrals for physical dynamics. MD-, normal-mode-, experimentally supported-, or otherwise validated ensemble generation is preferred.

Permutation-invariant conformer pooling feeds the Equiformer-Light backbone and allows downstream planning to condition on rigid regions, flexible loops and alternative pocket states.
