# 5.1. Foundations of Lie Groups and Lie Algebras

SynPath-3D uses \(\mathrm{SO}(3)\) and \(\mathrm{SE}(3)\) for spatial orientation and rigid-transform propagation.

## Canonical application

- \(\mathrm{SE}(3)\): rigid spatial transformations;
- \(\mathfrak{se}(3)\): screw/twist coordinates for PoE;
- \(\mathbb T^K\): periodic rotatable-bond coordinates;
- bounded Euclidean variables: selected valence-angle compliance.

For CPDF targets, a scalar field gradient does not uniquely determine a full SO(3) frame. Directional targets therefore require additional local geometric information or a learned directional head.

General Lie-group material in this note is foundational theory. The exact molecular parameterization is defined by the canonical CPDF, torus-flow and compliant-PoE notes.
