# 5.1. Foundations of Lie Groups and Lie Algebras

SynPath-3D uses \(\mathrm{SO}(3)\) and \(\mathrm{SE}(3)\) as geometric tools for molecular coordinate propagation.

## Canonical application

- \(\mathrm{SE}(3)\) represents rigid spatial transforms;
- \(\mathfrak{se}(3)\) provides twist coordinates used by the PoE kinematics engine;
- \(\mathbb T^K\) represents periodic rotatable-bond coordinates;
- bounded Euclidean compliance variables represent selected valence-angle deviations.

For CPDF target orientations, a single scalar field gradient does not determine a unique rotation matrix. The implementation therefore uses additional local geometry or a learned directional representation before constructing \(\mathrm{SO}(3)\) frames.

General Lie-group theory in this note is educational; the canonical molecular implementation is defined by the kinematics and CPDF module notes.
