# 5.3. Energy-Guided Kinematic Steric Avoidance

Steric guidance is integrated into the torus flow rather than applied as a separate post-hoc DLS correction.

\[
v_{\mathrm{guided}}
=
v_\theta
-
\eta(s)J^\top\nabla_xV_{\mathrm{rep}}.
\]

The Jacobian maps Cartesian repulsive forces back into internal-coordinate velocity space.

DLS and MMFF94s remain useful comparison baselines, but they are not canonical generation stages.
