# 7.1. Torus Flow Matching with Analytical Energy Guidance

Rotatable-bond configurations live on the flat torus:

\[
\mathbb T^K=[-\pi,\pi)^K.
\]

Training uses wrapped geodesic displacement. Sampling uses an analytical pocket-repulsion guidance term projected through the kinematic Jacobian.

The four-step midpoint solver is the initial fast configuration and must be validated against higher-step references.
