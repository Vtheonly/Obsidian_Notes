# 5.2.1. Compliant Lie-Algebraic Screw Theory and Elastic Valence Mechanics

The molecular state is:

\[
\Theta=[\phi\in\mathbb T^K,\delta\theta\in\mathbb R^M].
\]

Torsional coordinates are periodic. Selected valence-angle deviations are bounded:

\[
|\delta\theta_m|\le0.07\text{ rad}.
\]

They receive explicit elastic regularization:

\[
E_{\mathrm{elastic}}
=
\frac12\sum_m k_{\theta,m}\delta\theta_m^2.
\]

The PoE transforms preserve declared covalent bond lengths. The Jacobian contains torsional and compliant-angle columns and maps Cartesian steric guidance into internal-coordinate velocity space.

Chemical planarity and stereochemistry are provided by explicit reaction/ligand constraints rather than one universal hard-coded torsion.
