# 6.1. Ensemble-Conditioned Equivariant Pocket Backbone

The canonical backbone is a six-layer Equiformer-Light model operating on 3–5 receptor conformations.

\[
X_P\in\mathbb R^{K\times N_p\times3}.
\]

The network produces scalar width 256 and vector channels \(3\times64\), followed by permutation-invariant conformer pooling.

Stage 0 pretraining uses masked residue recovery and coordinate denoising on a large structural corpus.

The backbone is the shared structural representation for HENF, CPDF and the planner.
