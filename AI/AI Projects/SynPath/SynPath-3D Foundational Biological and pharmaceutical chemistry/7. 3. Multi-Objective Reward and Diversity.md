# 7.3. Hard-Gated Consensus Reward and Diversity

The terminal reward first checks physical and synthesis validity.

\[
G(\mathcal M)=I_{\mathrm{physical}}I_{\mathrm{synth}}.
\]

Failed candidates receive the numerical floor. Valid candidates receive the calibrated consensus score built from interaction, desolvation, hydration/dewetting, strain and diversity.

The diversity term discourages a single scaffold family from dominating the sampled distribution.

No additive quality term may compensate for failed validity gates.
