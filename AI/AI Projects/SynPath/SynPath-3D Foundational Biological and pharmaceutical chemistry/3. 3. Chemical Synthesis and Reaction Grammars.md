# 3.3. Chemical Synthesis and Reaction Grammars

SynPath-3D models synthesis as a finite certified reaction grammar operating over an inventory of curated building blocks.

## Synthesis state

Functional handles are represented by explicit finite-state bitsets. The production system does not use a DPDA stack.

## Action

\[
a_t=(r_t,B_t),
\]

where \(r_t\) is one of the 24 certified reaction classes and \(B_t\) is selected from the reaction-compatible catalog through hierarchical retrieval.

The discrete action pipeline is:

\[
\text{legal reaction}
\rightarrow
\text{continuous target profile}
\rightarrow
\text{ANN retrieval}
\rightarrow
\text{contextual re-ranking}.
\]

Route feasibility is computationally constrained by the declared reaction grammar and inventory, but this does not constitute a guarantee of laboratory success.

The 75k catalog is a project curation target. Its exact contents, coverage and availability are defined by the frozen catalog manifest.

Large synthetic-route corpora teach reaction compatibility and retrieval behavior; approximate docked coordinates are not ground-truth 3D geometry.
