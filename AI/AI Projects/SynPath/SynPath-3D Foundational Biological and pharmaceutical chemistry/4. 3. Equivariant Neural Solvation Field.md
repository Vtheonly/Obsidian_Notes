# 4.3. Hydration Energy Neural Field

HENF is the canonical continuous solvent field for SynPath-3D.

\[
(\mathcal P,r)\mapsto[\Delta\mu_{\mathrm{excess}}(r),g_O(r)].
\]

The field is conditioned on the pocket representation produced from an ensemble of receptor conformations. A dual-scale design combines a continuum GB/SA component with a learned local hydration/dewetting field.

3D-RISM remains useful for theory and auxiliary teacher data, but it is not required at runtime.

Thresholds for frustrated-water classification are calibrated experimentally or against held-out hydration references.
