# 2.2. Protein Cavities and Binding Sites

## 2.2.1 Pocket Topography

A binding pocket is a target-specific geometric and chemical environment. Pocket volume, depth, buriedness and flexibility vary substantially across targets and are dataset-dependent descriptors.

Cryptic pockets may require conformational transitions before a ligand can bind. A static apo structure therefore does not define the complete accessible binding landscape.

## 2.2.2 Protonation

Ionization states depend on local environment. Physiological-pH defaults are useful preprocessing priors, but buried/catalytic environments can shift pKa values. Protonation tools and parameters are recorded in the dataset manifest.

## 2.2.3 Multi-Conformer Conditioning

SynPath-3D represents a target pocket by a correlated ensemble:

\[
\mathcal P=\{X_P^{(1)},\ldots,X_P^{(K)}\},
\qquad K\in[3,5].
\]

Independent Gaussian perturbations of isolated sidechain torsions are **not** the canonical receptor-dynamics model.

Conformer pooling must be permutation-invariant and retain both consensus geometry and flexibility descriptors such as ensemble positional variance/RMSF.

This representation feeds the Equiformer-Light backbone and is the canonical basis for downstream HENF, CPDF and planning.
