# 4.3.1. Dual-Scale Solvation Thermodynamics: GIST Dewetting and Continuum Polarization

SynPath-3D separates smooth long-range solvent response from localized interfacial hydration.

## Continuum component

A GB/SA-style model provides a differentiable approximation of solvent screening and polarization.

\[
\Delta G_{\mathrm{solv}}
\approx
\Delta G_{\mathrm{polar}}^{\mathrm{GB}}
+
\Delta G_{\mathrm{local}}.
\]

Dielectric constants, atomic radii and scaling factors are implementation parameters and must be pinned in the experiment manifest.

## Local component

GIST/WaterMap-style explicit-solvent calculations provide teacher signals for local density, orientational ordering and hydration thermodynamics. HENF learns a continuous representation:

\[
\Psi_\phi(r\mid\mathcal P)
\rightarrow
[\Delta\mu_{\mathrm{excess}}(r),g_O(r)].
\]

Frustrated-water and structural-water thresholds are calibration parameters, not universal constants.

3D-RISM is an optional reference/teacher method, not the required production inference path.
