# 4.3.1. Dual-Scale Solvation Thermodynamics: GIST Dewetting and Continuum Polarization

Binding in aqueous solution includes both solute-solvent and solute-solute contributions.

A useful decomposition is:

\[
\Delta G_{\mathrm{solvation}}
=
\Delta G_{\mathrm{polar}}^{\mathrm{continuum}}
+
\Delta G_{\mathrm{interfacial}}.
\]

The continuum component captures long-range dielectric screening. A GB-style model can provide a differentiable approximation to polar solvation.

The interfacial component describes local water structure, including density, orientation and energetic frustration near the pocket.

## Role in SynPath-3D

HENF is the learned runtime approximation to local hydration information. GIST/WaterMap-style explicit-solvent observables can supervise or benchmark it. 3D-RISM remains a reference/auxiliary method.

No fixed energy threshold should be treated as a universal boundary between “frustrated” and “structural” water. Thresholds are calibration parameters tied to a defined teacher and benchmark.

## Important distinction

This physical note describes the decomposition of solvation thermodynamics. The production software module is HENF. It should query a continuous field rather than require a precomputed grid for every generated ligand.
