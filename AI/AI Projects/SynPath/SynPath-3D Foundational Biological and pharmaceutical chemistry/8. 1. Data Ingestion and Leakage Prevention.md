# 8.1. Data Ingestion, Stage-0 Pretraining, and Leakage Prevention

SynPath-3D separates structural pretraining, experimental SBDD geometry, synthetic chemistry routes and held-out evaluation.

Stage 0 uses a large PDB-derived corpus. Stage 1 uses purified experimental trajectories plus a larger synthetic-route corpus for discrete chemistry/retrieval learning.

Leakage controls cover protein sequence, protein structure, exact molecule and chemical scaffold similarity.

MMseqs2, Foldseek and Bemis-Murcko filtering remain canonical.
