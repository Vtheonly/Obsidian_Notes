# 7.2. Pruned Sub-Trajectory Balance GFlowNets

Stage 2 uses SubTB over the explicitly defined legal/retrieved action support.

\[
\mathcal A_t=
\mathcal A_t^{\mathrm{legal}}
\cap
\mathcal A_t^{\mathrm{retrieved}}.
\]

The initial synthon retrieval support is capped at 128 candidates. Entropy regularization is applied to the forward policy over that support.

The action-pruning operation is part of the trained policy, not an external filter.
