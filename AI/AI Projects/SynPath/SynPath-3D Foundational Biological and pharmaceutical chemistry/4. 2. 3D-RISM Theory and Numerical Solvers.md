# 4.2. 3D-RISM Theory and Numerical Solvers

3D-RISM is retained as a theoretical and computational reference method.

## Role in SynPath-3D

3D-RISM may be used for:

- methodological comparison;
- auxiliary hydration teacher data;
- closure-sensitivity studies.

It is not the mandatory production runtime.

The production runtime interface is HENF:

\[
(\mathcal P,r)\rightarrow
[\Delta\mu_{\mathrm{excess}}(r),g_O(r)].
\]

## Numerical theory

3D-RISM solves three-dimensional solvent correlation equations on a spatial grid together with a chosen closure. KH remains useful for controlled comparison, but its output must not be conflated with experimental hydration free energy.

Any experiment using 3D-RISM records solvent model, closure, grid spacing, convergence and compute cost.

Statements that SynPath-3D routinely processes 2,500 production pockets through 3D-RISM are obsolete.
