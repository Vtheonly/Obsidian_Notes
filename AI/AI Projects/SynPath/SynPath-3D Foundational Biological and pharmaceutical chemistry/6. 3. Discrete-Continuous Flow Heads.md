# 6.3. Hierarchical Discrete and Continuous Flow Heads

The shared planning state drives two coupled branches.

### Discrete branch

\[
z_t
\rightarrow
24\text{-class legal reaction}
\rightarrow
q_{\mathrm{target}}\in\mathbb R^{263}
\rightarrow
\text{top-128 retrieval}
\rightarrow
\text{contextual re-ranking}.
\]

### Continuous branch

The selected synthon is placed into the compliant kinematic state and generated through energy-guided torus flow.

The branches communicate through the planning state and target-conditioned context rather than a flat joint action space.
