# 6.2. Continuous Pharmacophore Density Field

The old fixed-anchor formulation is replaced by a continuous six-channel field:

\[
\Phi_\theta(r\mid\mathcal P)\in[0,1]^6.
\]

Channels encode donor, acceptor, aromatic, hydrophobic, cationic and anionic interaction targets.

Spatial maxima are extracted adaptively, with an initial decoder budget \(1\le M\le12\).

CPDF supplies distal geometric goals to the synthesis planner. Directional frames require more information than a single scalar-field gradient.
