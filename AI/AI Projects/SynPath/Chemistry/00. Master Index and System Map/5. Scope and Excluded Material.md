# 5. Scope and Excluded Material

> [!abstract] Core Idea
> This vault covers exactly what the 3D-SynTree project uses — nothing more. Several conventional academic topics have been deliberately verified as having no application to the project and are excluded to protect your study time. This page records the exclusions so you never wonder whether you are missing something.

## 1. What Is In Scope

Ten modules, mapped one-to-one onto the components of the system (see [[1. 3D-SynTree System Overview]]):

| Domain | Coverage |
| --- | --- |
| Chemistry foundations | Atomic structure, bonding, hybridization, functional groups, resonance, pH 7.4 ionization — the language of the action space |
| Medicinal chemistry and synthesis | Synthons, the 32 certified reactions, SMARTS and isotope provenance, chemoselectivity, Fsp3, Enamine REAL |
| Spatial chemistry and force fields | Stereochemistry, dihedrals, steric clash, Lennard-Jones, ETKDG, MMFF94, Kabsch alignment — the conformer engine |

| Geometric deep learning | Groups, equivariance, radial bases, PaiNN, bipartite graphs, cross-attention — the encoder |
| Circular statistics | SO(2) statistics, von Mises, Bessel and Amos, torsion bin design — the torsion head |
| MDPs and GFlowNets | Assembly MDP, trajectory balance, backward flows, stationary reward, curriculum — the learner |
| Benchmarking and translation | PoseBusters, AiZynthFinder, baselines, and the wet-lab assay cascade |

## 2. Explicitly Excluded Concepts

The following conventional academic topics are confirmed to have **no application** to 3D-SynTree. Skip them without guilt:

- **Inorganic and classical physics** — transition-metal coordination and crystal field theory (the project's molecules are purely organic), inorganic metallurgy, quantum electrodynamics, mineralogy, and nuclear decay kinetics (beyond knowing that isotope labels exist and do not change reactivity — see [[3. Reaction SMARTS and Isotope Provenance]]).
- **Non-target biology** — cell mitosis and meiosis, developmental biology, organismal zoology, plant physiology, ecology, and classical Mendelian population genetics. The project touches biology only at the level of protein structure and binding.
- **Off-target synthetic chemistry** — organometallic Stille and Heck couplings (deliberately dropped from the certified grammar; Suzuki and Sonogashira cover the needed C–C couplings), total synthesis of complex natural macrolides, photoredox radical chemistry, and polymer extrusion mechanics. The system runs 32 robust medicinal-chemistry reactions, not frontier synthesis.
- **Irrelevant medical disciplines** — clinical trial statistical design (Phase II/III epidemiology), surgical pathology, medical diagnostics, and hospital patient management. The project stops at molecule design and biophysical validation; it never enters the clinic.

## 4. Why Exclusions Matter

The purpose of this vault is complete understanding of **this project**, not a chemistry or pharmacology degree. Every excluded topic was checked against the codebase's actual dependencies — data formats, reaction grammar, force fields, encoders, losses, rewards, and evaluation pipelines — and rejected only when nothing in the system depends on it. If a future version of the project grows a dependency (for example, metalloprotein cofactors or covalent docking), the expansion protocol in [[4. Vault Conventions and Note Schema]] adds the missing notes without disturbing the rest.
