## Terminology policy

- **HENF** is the production hydration module. **ENSF** is a legacy alias only.
- **CPDF** is the production target field. **TIF** is a legacy alias only.
- **Bitmask tracker** is the production reaction-state system. **DPDA** is legacy/ablation terminology.
- **Energy-guided torus flow** is the production geometry generator.
- **DLS/MMFF94s** may appear only as validation or ablation baselines.
- **HNSW/FAISS** are implementation choices under the ANN abstraction. Do not claim GPU HNSW specifically.
- Numeric latency, memory, dataset-size and performance values are targets or measured results only when linked to an experiment manifest.

## Contradiction rule

When a lower-level or older note conflicts with this contract, the contract and the current canonical component note win. The conflicting note must be rewritten as either:

1. a corrected canonical-reference note; or
2. an explicitly labeled legacy/ablation note.

No two notes may define different production interfaces for the same component.
