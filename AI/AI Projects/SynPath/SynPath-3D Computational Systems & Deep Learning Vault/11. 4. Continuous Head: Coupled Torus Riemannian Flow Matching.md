# 11.4. Energy-Guided Coupled Torus Flow Matching

Rotatable bonds are modeled on \(\mathbb T^K\).

\[
\gamma_s=\phi_0+s\,\operatorname{wrap}(\phi_1-\phi_0).
\]

Generation uses:

\[
v_{\mathrm{guided}}
=
v_\theta
-
\eta(s)J^\top\nabla_xV_{\mathrm{rep}}.
\]

A four-step midpoint solver is the default fast sampler.

Compliant angle variables are handled by the PoE kinematics module. Mandatory DLS correction and MMFF94s minimization are no longer generation stages.

Four ODE steps and any latency target must be empirically validated.
