# 11.4. Energy-Guided Coupled Torus Flow Matching

The state is:

\[
\Theta=[\phi,\delta\theta],
\qquad
\dim(\Theta)=K+M.
\]

The flow head predicts:

\[
v_\Theta=[v_\phi,v_{\delta\theta}].
\]

The physical guidance term uses the complete compliant Jacobian:

\[
v_{\Theta,\mathrm{guided}}
=
v_\Theta-\eta(s)J(\Theta)^\top\nabla_xV_{\mathrm{rep}}.
\]

Torsions are wrapped modulo \(2\pi\); compliant angles are clamped to their configured bounds.

A four-step explicit midpoint solver is the default fast configuration. Validate it against higher-step references.

There is no production DLS correction or MMFF94s minimization stage.
