# 11.4. Energy-Guided Coupled Torus Flow Matching

The joint state is:

\[
\Theta=[\phi,\delta\theta],
\qquad
v_\Theta=[v_\phi,v_{\delta\theta}].
\]

Physical guidance is:

\[
v_{\Theta,\mathrm{guided}}
=
v_\Theta-
\eta(s)J(\Theta)^\top\nabla_xV_{\mathrm{rep}}.
\]

Here \(J\in\mathbb R^{3N\times(K+M)}\).

Torsions are wrapped on the flat torus. Compliant angles are integrated in Euclidean coordinates and clamped to configured bounds.

The initial sampler is four-step explicit midpoint. This is a runtime starting point and must be validated against higher-step references.

No DLS or MMFF94s stage is hidden inside production generation.
