### 1. Concept Definition & Intuition
When generating molecular dihedrals $\vec{\phi} \in \mathbb{T}^K = [-\pi, \pi)^K$, standard Gaussian flow matching in Euclidean space fails because the domain is periodic.

**Riemannian Optimal Transport Flow Matching on $\mathbb{T}^K$** constructs linear probability paths along circular geodesics. 

Given a base sample $\vec{\phi}_0 \sim \mathcal{U}([-\pi, \pi)^K)$ and a ground-truth crystal dihedral $\vec{\phi}_1 \in [-\pi, \pi)^K$:
1. Calculate the target velocity as the wrapped angular displacement:
   $$\mathbf{u}^* = \text{wrap}(\vec{\phi}_1 - \vec{\phi}_0) = (\vec{\phi}_1 - \vec{\phi}_0 + \pi \pmod{2\pi}) - \pi \in [-\pi, \pi)^K$$
2. The conditional probability path is:
   $$\vec{\phi}_t = (\vec{\phi}_0 + t \mathbf{u}^* + \pi \pmod{2\pi}) - \pi$$
3. The regression loss trains the network $\mathbf{v}_\theta(\vec{\phi}_t, t)$ to predict $\mathbf{u}^*$:
   $$\mathcal{L}_{\text{torus}} = \mathbb{E}_{t, \vec{\phi}_0, \vec{\phi}_1} \left[ \sum_{k=1}^K \min\left( |v_k(\vec{\phi}_t, t) - u_k^*|, \, 2\pi - |v_k(\vec{\phi}_t, t) - u_k^*| \right)^2 \right]$$
