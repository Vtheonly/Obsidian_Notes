# 11.2. Continuous Pharmacophore Density Field (CPDF)

The canonical teleological module predicts a continuous six-channel interaction field rather than exactly five anchors.

\[
\Phi_\theta(r\mid P)\in[0,1]^6.
\]

The six channels represent donor, acceptor, aromatic, hydrophobic, cationic and anionic interaction targets.

At inference, spatial maxima are extracted with NMS or clustering. The number of returned targets is adaptive with an initial decoder budget \(1\le M\le12\).

A scalar gradient is not sufficient to define a unique SO(3) frame; directional target frames therefore require additional local geometric information or a learned directional head.

This module provides distal planning goals to the chemical policy.
