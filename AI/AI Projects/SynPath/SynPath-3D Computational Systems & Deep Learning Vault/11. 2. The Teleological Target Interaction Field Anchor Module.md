# 11.2. Continuous Pharmacophore Density Field (CPDF)

CPDF predicts:

\[
\Phi_\theta(r)\in[0,1]^6
\]

for donor, acceptor, aromatic, hydrophobic, cationic and anionic target densities.

The field is queried over the cavity and decoded into spatial maxima with NMS or clustering. \(M_{\max}=12\) is an initial computational decoder cap, not a biological law.

A scalar field gradient provides only one direction and cannot by itself define a unique SO(3) frame. Directional target frames require additional local geometry, higher-order derivatives or a learned orientation head.

Ground truth is built from chemically typed interaction neighborhoods around experimental ligand poses. CPDF is a distal planning representation, not a direct claim that every predicted maximum is a realized interaction.
