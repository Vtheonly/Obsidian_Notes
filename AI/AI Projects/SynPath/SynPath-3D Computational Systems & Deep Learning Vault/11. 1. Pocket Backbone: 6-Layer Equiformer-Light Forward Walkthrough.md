# 11.1. Pocket Backbone: 6-Layer Equiformer-Light Forward Walkthrough

The forward pass begins with 3–5 receptor conformers rather than one rigid crystal.

\[
X_P\in\mathbb R^{K\times N_p\times3},\quad K\in[3,5].
\]

Each conformer supplies element identity, charge/context features, surface descriptors and local geometry.

The six-layer Equiformer-Light backbone produces:

\[
h_p\in\mathbb R^{N_p\times256},
\qquad
v_p\in\mathbb R^{N_p\times3\times64}.
\]

An invariant conformer-pooling stage retains both consensus structure and flexibility information.

Stage 0 initializes the backbone using masked-residue recovery and coordinate denoising on a large PDB-derived corpus.

HENF and CPDF consume the resulting pocket representation downstream.
