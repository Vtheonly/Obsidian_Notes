# 13.2. Pruned Sub-Trajectory Balance for Variable-Length Synthesis

Stage 2 trains on legal actions intersected with the hierarchical retrieved support.

\[
\mathcal A_t=\mathcal A_t^{\mathrm{legal}}\cap\mathcal A_t^{\mathrm{retrieved}}.
\]

Use:

\[
\mathcal L_{\mathrm{SubTB}}
=
\sum_{i<j}\lambda^{j-i-1}\Delta_{i,j}^2,
\qquad \lambda=0.90
\]

plus trajectory entropy regularization.

The 128-candidate budget defines the practical action support. STOP/deprotection are included only when explicitly legal.

Initialize from Stage 1 and monitor entropy, log Z, invalid trajectories and scaffold diversity.
