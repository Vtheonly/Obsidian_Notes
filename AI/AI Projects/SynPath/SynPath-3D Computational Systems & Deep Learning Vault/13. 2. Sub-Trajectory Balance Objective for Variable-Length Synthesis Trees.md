### 1. Mathematical Formulation
During Stage 2 alignment, the model optimizes the Sub-Trajectory Balance objective ($\text{SubTB}(\lambda)$) over complete synthesis DAGs:
$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{SubTB}}(\tau) = \sum_{0 \le i < j \le T} \lambda^{j - i - 1} \left( \log F_\theta(s_i) + \sum_{t=i}^{j-1} \log P_{F,\theta}(a_t \mid s_t, \mathcal{Y}^*) - \log F_\theta(s_j) \right)^2$$
Boundary conditions:
* $\log F_\theta(s_0) \equiv \log Z_\theta$ (learnable scalar parameter).
* $\log F(s_T) \equiv \log R(\mathcal{M})$ (terminal thermodynamic reward).
* $\lambda = 0.90$ (geometric discount factor).
