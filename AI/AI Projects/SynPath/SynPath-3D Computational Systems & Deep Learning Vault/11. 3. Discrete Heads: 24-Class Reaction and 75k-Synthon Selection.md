# 11.3. Discrete Heads: Reaction Selection and Hierarchical Synthon Retrieval

The discrete policy is hierarchical.

\[
z_t
\rightarrow
24\text{-class legal reaction}
\rightarrow
q_{\mathrm{target}}\in\mathbb R^{263}
\rightarrow
\operatorname{Top128}
\rightarrow
\text{contextual re-ranking}.
\]

The 24 reaction classes are masked by functional-handle legality.

The target vector contains a 256-D chemical embedding target plus exit-vector, volume and electronic descriptors.

The 75k catalog is indexed with precomputed descriptors. ANN lookup is non-differentiable; metric learning trains the query space and the re-ranker learns pocket-conditioned preference.

The old flat 75k-way classifier is an ablation baseline only.
