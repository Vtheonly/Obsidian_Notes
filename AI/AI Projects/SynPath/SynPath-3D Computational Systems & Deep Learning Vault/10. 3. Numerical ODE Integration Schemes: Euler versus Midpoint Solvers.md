### 1. Mathematical Mechanics & Error Analysis
At inference time, the trajectory $\vec{\phi}(1)$ is evaluated by integrating $\frac{d\vec{\phi}}{dt} = \mathbf{v}_\theta(\vec{\phi}_t, t)$ over $t \in [0, 1]$ from initial noise $\vec{\phi}_0$:

* **First-Order Euler Method ($N$ steps, $\Delta t = 1/N$)**:
  $$\vec{\phi}_{n+1} = \text{wrap}\left( \vec{\phi}_n + \Delta t \, \mathbf{v}_\theta(\vec{\phi}_n, t_n) \right)$$
  * Truncation error: Local $\mathcal{O}(\Delta t^2)$, Global $\mathcal{O}(\Delta t)$.
  * *Failure Mode*: Curvature in the velocity field causes Euler integration to overshoot local minima, resulting in angular errors of $15^\circ\text{ to }25^\circ$ when $N \le 4$.
* **Second-Order Runge-Kutta (Midpoint) Solver ($N$ steps, $\Delta t = 1/N$)**:
  $$\mathbf{k}_1 = \mathbf{v}_\theta(\vec{\phi}_n, t_n)$$
  $$\vec{\phi}_{\text{mid}} = \text{wrap}\left( \vec{\phi}_n + \frac{\Delta t}{2} \mathbf{k}_1 \right)$$
  $$\mathbf{k}_2 = \mathbf{v}_\theta\left( \vec{\phi}_{\text{mid}}, \, t_n + \frac{\Delta t}{2} \right)$$
  $$\vec{\phi}_{n+1} = \text{wrap}\left( \vec{\phi}_n + \Delta t \, \mathbf{k}_2 \right)$$
  * Truncation error: Local $\mathcal{O}(\Delta t^3)$, Global $\mathcal{O}(\Delta t^2)$.
  * *Result*: For $N=4$ steps (8 neural function evaluations), the midpoint solver reduces angular error to $< 1.5^\circ$, satisfying the accuracy requirements for downstream steric packing.
