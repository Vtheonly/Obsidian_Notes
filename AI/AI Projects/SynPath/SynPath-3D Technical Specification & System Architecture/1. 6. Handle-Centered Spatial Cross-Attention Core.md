# 1.6. Handle-Centered Spatial Cross-Attention Core

The planning core fuses the current molecular state, reacting handle, pocket ensemble representation, HENF signals and active CPDF targets.

## Spatial attention

\[
A_{ij}
=
\frac{q_i^\top k_j}{\sqrt d}
+
b(\|x_i-x_j\|).
\]

The distance bias is invariant to global translation and rotation.

## Planning state

\[
z_t\in\mathbb R^{256}.
\]

The shared planning state feeds:

- the legal 24-class reaction head;
- the 263-D target-profile head;
- the continuous flow-conditioning path.

The CPDF targets give the policy explicit distal spatial objectives, reducing reliance on myopic local growth.
