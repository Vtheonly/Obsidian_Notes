# 3.1. Stage-1 Multi-Task Objective with Metric InfoNCE Loss

\[
\mathcal L_1 =
\lambda_{\mathrm{cpdf}}\mathcal L_{\mathrm{cpdf}}+
\lambda_{\mathrm{rxn}}\mathcal L_{\mathrm{rxn}}+
\lambda_{\mathrm{target}}\mathcal L_{\mathrm{target}}+
\lambda_{\mathrm{flow}}\mathcal L_{\mathrm{flow}}+
\lambda_{\mathrm{elastic}}\mathcal L_{\mathrm{elastic}}.
\]

Kendall-Gal homoscedastic weighting may parameterize the task coefficients, but the learned variances must be monitored.

## CPDF

Use focal/asymmetric BCE over chemically typed spatial interaction targets:

\[
\mathcal L_{\mathrm{cpdf}}=\operatorname{FocalBCE}(\Phi_\theta,\Phi^*).
\]

## Reaction

\[
\mathcal L_{\mathrm{rxn}}
=
-\alpha_{r^*}(1-p_{r^*})^\gamma\log p_{r^*},
\qquad \gamma\approx2.
\]

Weights come from the observed reaction-frequency distribution.

## Retrieval target

For query q and positive synthon k+:

\[
\mathcal L_{\mathrm{InfoNCE}}
=
-\log
\frac{e^{\operatorname{sim}(q,k^+)/\tau}}
{e^{\operatorname{sim}(q,k^+)/\tau}
+\sum_{k\in\mathcal N}e^{\operatorname{sim}(q,k)/\tau}}.
\]

Similarity combines normalized chemical embedding, exit-vector and size compatibility.

## Torus flow

\[
\mathcal L_{\mathrm{flow}}
=
\mathbb E[(v_\theta-u^*)^2]
+
\lambda_{\mathrm{elastic}}\|\delta\theta\|^2.
\]

All angular differences are wrapped on the torus.

Track component losses, retrieval recall@K, reaction accuracy, CPDF calibration and geometric validity independently.
