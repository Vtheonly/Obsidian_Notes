# 3.1. Stage-1 Multi-Task Objective with Metric InfoNCE Loss

\[
\mathcal L_1=
\lambda_c\mathcal L_{\mathrm{CPDF}}+
\lambda_r\mathcal L_{\mathrm{rxn}}+
\lambda_t\mathcal L_{\mathrm{InfoNCE}}+
\lambda_f\mathcal L_{\mathrm{flow}}+
\lambda_e\mathcal L_{\mathrm{elastic}}.
\]

## CPDF

Use a class-imbalanced focal/asymmetric BCE objective over sampled spatial interaction targets.

## Reaction

\[
\mathcal L_{\mathrm{rxn}}
=
-\alpha_{r^*}(1-p_{r^*})^\gamma\log p_{r^*},
\qquad \gamma\approx2.
\]

## Retrieval

For a positive synthon descriptor \(k^+\) and negatives \(\mathcal N\):

\[
\mathcal L_{\mathrm{InfoNCE}}
=
-\log
\frac{\exp(\operatorname{sim}(q,k^+)/\tau)}
{\exp(\operatorname{sim}(q,k^+)/\tau)+
\sum_{k\in\mathcal N}\exp(\operatorname{sim}(q,k)/\tau)}.
\]

Similarity combines normalized chemical embedding compatibility with exit-vector and size terms.

## Flow matching

For torsions:

\[
\mathcal L_{\phi}
=
\mathbb E
\left[
\frac1K
\sum_{k=1}^{K}
\left(\operatorname{wrap}(v_{\phi,k}-u_{\phi,k}^*)\right)^2
\right].
\]

For compliant-angle velocities:

\[
\mathcal L_{\delta\theta}
=
\mathbb E
\left[
\frac1M\|v_{\delta\theta}-u_{\delta\theta}^*\|_2^2
\right].
\]

Therefore:

\[
\mathcal L_{\mathrm{flow}}
=
\mathcal L_{\phi}
+
\mathcal L_{\delta\theta}.
\]

Elastic geometry regularization is explicit:

\[
\mathcal L_{\mathrm{elastic}}
=
\frac12\delta\theta^\top K_\theta\delta\theta.
\]

Task coefficients may be balanced with Kendall-Gal homoscedastic weighting. Track every component independently.
