# 3.2. Stage-2 Pruned Sub-Trajectory Balance (SubTB) GFlowNet Objective

For sub-trajectory i→j:

\[
\Delta_{i,j}
=
\log F_\theta(s_i)+
\sum_{t=i}^{j-1}\log P_F(a_t|s_t)-
\log F_\theta(s_j).
\]

\[
\mathcal L_{\mathrm{SubTB}}
=
\sum_{i<j}\lambda^{j-i-1}\Delta_{i,j}^2,
\qquad \lambda=0.90.
\]

## Actual action support

\[
\mathcal A_t=
\mathcal A_t^{\mathrm{legal}}
\cap
\mathcal A_t^{\mathrm{retrieved}}.
\]

The retrieved support is capped at 128 candidates, with legal auxiliary actions such as STOP or deprotection when applicable.

The forward policy is renormalized over this support. Pruning is therefore part of the model definition during Stage 2.

## Entropy regularization

\[
\mathcal L_2
=
\mathcal L_{\mathrm{SubTB}}
-
\alpha_{\mathrm{ent}}
\sum_t\mathcal H(P_F(\cdot|s_t)).
\]

An initial alpha such as 0.01 and a decay schedule may be tested; it is not a fixed scientific constant.

Initialize Stage 2 from a converged Stage-1 checkpoint and monitor log Z, invalid trajectories, entropy and scaffold diversity.
