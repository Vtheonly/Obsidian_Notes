# 1.8. Energy-Guided Coupled Torus Flow Matching Head

The continuous state is:

\[
\Theta=[\phi,\delta\theta],
\qquad
\dim(\Theta)=K+M.
\]

The learned velocity has matching dimension:

\[
v_\Theta=[v_\phi,v_{\delta\theta}]\in\mathbb R^{K+M}.
\]

The production guidance term is:

\[
v_{\Theta,\mathrm{guided}}
=
v_\Theta-\eta(s)J(\Theta)^\top\nabla_xV_{\mathrm{rep}}.
\]

Here \(J\in\mathbb R^{3N\times(K+M)}\), so the projected guidance and learned velocity are dimensionally compatible.

Torsions are integrated on the flat torus with wrapping. Compliant angles are updated in Euclidean coordinates and clamped to the configured bounds.

The initial sampler is a four-step explicit midpoint ODE. Step count and latency are empirical runtime parameters.

DLS and MMFF94s are comparison/validation baselines, not hidden generation stages.
