# 1.8. Energy-Guided Coupled Torus Flow Matching Head

Rotatable bonds are represented on:

\[
\mathbb T^K=[-\pi,\pi)^K.
\]

The base-to-target conditional path is:

\[
\gamma_s=\phi_0+s\,\operatorname{wrap}(\phi_1-\phi_0).
\]

Sampling uses:

\[
v_{\mathrm{guided}}
=
v_\theta(\phi_s,s,z_t,E_B)
-
\eta(s)J(\Theta_s)^\top\nabla_xV_{\mathrm{rep}}(x(\Theta_s)).
\]

The default fast sampler is a four-step explicit midpoint ODE. The number of steps and latency are empirical runtime parameters.

Compliant angle variables are handled by the PoE kinematics module.

Mandatory DLS correction and mandatory MMFF94s generation minimization are not production stages; they remain explicit validation baselines.
