# 6.1. Multi-Source Structural Biology Corpus Architecture

SynPath-3D uses separated data tiers for representation learning, experimental geometry, synthetic chemistry and final evaluation.

| Tier | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Structural PDB corpus | Stage-0 self-supervised pretraining |
| Purified experimental trajectories | Stage-1 3D grounding |
| Synthetic route corpus | Stage-1 reaction/retrieval pretraining |
| Held-out targets/scaffolds | validation |

Planning figures such as 100k+ structural examples, 30k experimental trajectories and 500k synthetic routes are targets for curation. The actual manifest is authoritative.

Every sample stores source, split, duplicate status, structural provenance, reaction path and label provenance.
