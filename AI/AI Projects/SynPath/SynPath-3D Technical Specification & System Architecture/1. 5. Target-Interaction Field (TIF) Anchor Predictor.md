# 1.5. Continuous Pharmacophore Density Field (CPDF) Module

CPDF replaces the fixed M=5 target-anchor formulation with a continuous six-channel spatial field.

\[
\Phi_\theta(r\mid\mathcal P)\in[0,1]^6.
\]

## Channels

1. hydrogen-bond donor target;
2. hydrogen-bond acceptor target;
3. aromatic interaction region;
4. hydrophobic target;
5. cationic target;
6. anionic target.

## Adaptive extraction

Evaluate the field over an internal spatial lattice or candidate point set and extract local maxima using NMS or equivalent clustering.

\[
\mathcal Y^*
=
\{(x_m^*,R_m^*,\tau_m^*,\Delta G_m^*)\}_{m=1}^{M}.
\]

An initial decoder budget of \(1\le M\le12\) is computational, not a biological law.

## Directional frames

A scalar density gradient alone does not uniquely determine an SO(3) frame. Directional frames must therefore use additional local geometry, pocket normals, derivatives or a learned directional head.

## Training

Ground-truth fields are derived from chemically typed interaction maps around experimentally resolved ligand poses. Use focal/asymmetric BCE or a compatible class-imbalanced point/voxel objective.

CPDF is a planning representation; a field maximum is not automatically an experimentally realized interaction.
