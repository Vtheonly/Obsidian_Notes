# 18.1. Controlled Experimental Protocols and Falsification Criteria

| Ablation | Remove/change | Measure |
|---|---|---|
| ABL-01 CPDF | fixed M=5 / blind growth baseline | target recall and geometric success |
| ABL-02 HENF | no learned hydration field | hydration calibration and downstream quality |
| ABL-03 compliant PoE | rigid-angle baseline | physical validity and strain |
| ABL-04 hierarchical retrieval | flat catalog baseline | recall@128 and diversity |
| ABL-05 pruned SubTB + gates | unpruned / soft reward | convergence and invalid-generation rate |

Also compare ensemble vs single-state conditioning, pretrained vs scratch backbone, guided vs unguided flow and four vs more ODE steps.

No percentage improvement is treated as established until reproduced on predefined held-out targets and scaffolds.
