# 3.3. Hard-Gated Consensus Thermodynamic Reward Functional

Validity and quality are separated.

\[
G(\mathcal M)=I_{\mathrm{PoseBusters}}I_{\mathrm{Synth}}.
\]

\[
R(\mathcal M)=
\begin{cases}
\epsilon,&G=0\\
\exp(-\Delta G_{\mathrm{consensus}}/E_0+\beta R_{\mathrm{div}}),&G=1.
\end{cases}
\]

Use a small numerical floor epsilon for stable logarithms while treating failed candidates as invalid.

## Consensus energy

\[
\Delta G_{\mathrm{consensus}}
=
w_{\mathrm{int}}\Delta H_{\mathrm{int}}
+w_{\mathrm{desolv}}\Delta G_{\mathrm{polar}}
-w_{\mathrm{water}}\Delta G_{\mathrm{dewet}}
+w_{\mathrm{strain}}\Delta E_{\mathrm{strain}}.
\]

Possible components include GNINA or another docking interaction term, polar desolvation, HENF hydration/dewetting and conformational strain.

These weights are calibrated model parameters, not a thermodynamic identity.

## Gates

PoseBusters uses the pinned installed version and its actual check set. AiZynthFinder uses the declared inventory and synthesis-depth configuration.

A failed physical or synthetic validity gate must not be compensated by a favorable docking score.

## Diversity

A scaffold/fingerprint diversity term prevents concentration into a single chemical series.

All reward components are independently audited on held-out and adversarial candidates.
