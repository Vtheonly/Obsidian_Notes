# 6.2. Ingestion Rules for Structural and Synthetic Training Data

## Experimental data

Apply structural-quality, ligand-validity, protonation, alternate-location, atom-mapping and duplicate filters before trajectory extraction.

## Synthetic chemistry data

Use large synthetic-route corpora to teach reaction grammar and discrete retrieval behavior. Approximate docked coordinates must not be silently promoted to ground-truth 3D flow targets.

## Leakage controls

Split independently by:

- protein sequence similarity;
- structural similarity;
- chemical scaffold;
- exact molecular identity.

MMseqs2, Foldseek and Bemis-Murcko filtering remain canonical.

Every sample carries a reproducible preprocessing and split manifest.
