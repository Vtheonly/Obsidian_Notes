## Configuration Rules

- The 3–5 conformer range is the canonical receptor-input design.
- The 75k catalog is indexed; it is not scored with a dense 75k-class softmax.
- CPDF has an adaptive anchor decoder; M=12 is a computational cap, not a biological constant.
- STOP is governed by explicit synthesis-state legality and termination criteria; molecular weight alone is not the termination rule.
- Four midpoint ODE steps are the initial fast configuration and must be validated against higher-step references.
- DLS/MMFF94s are external baselines, not production generation stages.
- Sample counts and thresholds in this file are planning defaults until frozen by a dataset/experiment manifest.
