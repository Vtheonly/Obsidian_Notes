# 1.9. Compliant Lie-Algebraic Screw Kinematics Engine

## State

\[
\Theta=[\phi\in\mathbb T^K,\delta\theta\in\mathbb R^M].
\]

A bounded compliance variable is used for selected valence angles:

\[
\delta\theta_m\in[-0.07,0.07]\text{ rad}.
\]

## Product of Exponentials

\[
T_a(\Theta)
=
\left(
\prod_{j\in\operatorname{anc}(a)}
\exp(\hat\xi_j\phi_j)
\exp(\hat\eta_j\delta\theta_j)
\right)
T_a(0).
\]

The rigid PoE transforms preserve the defined covalent bond lengths. Non-rigid chemistry must be represented through explicit compliant coordinates.

## Jacobian

\[
J(\Theta)
=
[J_{\mathrm{torsion}}\mid J_{\mathrm{angle}}].
\]

The Jacobian has \(3N\) Cartesian rows and \(K+M\) joint columns.

## Elastic penalty

\[
E_{\mathrm{elastic}}
=
\frac12\sum_m k_{\theta,m}\delta\theta_m^2.
\]

A nominal stiffness is an initialization parameter, not a universal molecular constant.

## Chemistry-specific geometry

Stereochemical and amide-planarity constraints come from the reaction/ligand representation. The kinematics engine must not impose one universal amide torsion.

Compliance is intended to prevent numerical kinematic dead ends while preserving chemical validity under external validation.
