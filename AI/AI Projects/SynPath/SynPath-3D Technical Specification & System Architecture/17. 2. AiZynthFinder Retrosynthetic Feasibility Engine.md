# 17.2. AiZynthFinder Retrosynthetic Feasibility Engine

Use AiZynthFinder to evaluate whether generated molecules can be mapped to an accepted retrosynthetic route under the configured reaction grammar, inventory and depth.

Inventory membership and a successful search are computational feasibility criteria, not experimental guarantees.

For Stage 2, synthesis feasibility is a hard gate: favorable docking or hydration scores cannot compensate for a failed route.

Report solve rate, route depth, route length, inventory coverage and failure reason.
