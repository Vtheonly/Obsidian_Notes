# 8.1. Diffraction Quality, Resolution, and R-Free Gateways

Crystal-structure quality filtering is configuration-driven.

## Baseline experimental SBDD profile

A strict profile may begin with:

- high structural resolution;
- acceptable \(R_{\mathrm{free}}\);
- complete ligand representation;
- valid heavy-atom geometry.

The project may use thresholds such as resolution ≤2.50 Å and \(R_{\mathrm{free}}\) ≤0.28 for a specific curated benchmark, but these values are **dataset-policy parameters**, not physical constants and must be recorded in the dataset manifest.

Stage-0 self-supervised structural pretraining may use a broader PDB-derived population than the stricter Stage-1 experimental geometry set.

Do not equate resolution with a universal per-atom coordinate-error threshold.
