# 22.1. Eighteen Foundational Questions and Definitive Solutions

## 1. What is the protein representation?
An ensemble of 3–5 pocket conformations encoded by a six-layer Equiformer-Light backbone with Stage-0 self-supervised initialization.

## 2. How is solvent represented?
By HENF, with explicit-solvent/GIST/GBSA references and optional 3D-RISM teacher data.

## 3. How are distal interaction goals represented?
By a continuous six-channel CPDF field with adaptive target extraction.

## 4. How is the next chemical action selected?
Legal 24-class reaction → 263-D target profile → top-128 reaction-compatible retrieval → contextual re-ranking.

## 5. How is reaction state tracked?
By explicit functional-handle bitsets plus provenance metadata.

## 6. What is the continuous geometry space?
Rotatable bonds live on the flat torus; bounded valence-angle compliance is an auxiliary Euclidean state.

## 7. How are steric clashes addressed?
Analytical repulsion is integrated into the flow field; DLS/MMFF94s are not mandatory generation stages.

## 8. How is the model trained?
Stage 0 structure pretraining, Stage 1 multi-task imitation/metric learning, Stage 2 pruned SubTB alignment.

## 9. What controls reward hacking?
Hard physical/synthesis gates before quality scoring.

## 10. What remains unresolved?
Hydration-field transfer, CPDF calibration, zero-shot retrieval, long-horizon synthesis quality and correlation between computational metrics and experimental activity.

Unresolved questions remain experimental questions, not assumptions hidden inside the architecture.
