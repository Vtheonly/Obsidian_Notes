# 17.3. Explicit-Solvent OpenMM MD Stability Harness

The baseline dynamical validation experiment is a 10 ns explicit-solvent OpenMM simulation with a pinned force field, water model, temperature, equilibration protocol and replicate policy.

## Report

- ligand RMSD;
- pocket-contact persistence;
- hydrogen-bond persistence where meaningful;
- severe steric events;
- ligand escape events;
- geometry degradation.

A stable 10 ns trajectory is short-timescale dynamical evidence, not a binding-free-energy measurement.
