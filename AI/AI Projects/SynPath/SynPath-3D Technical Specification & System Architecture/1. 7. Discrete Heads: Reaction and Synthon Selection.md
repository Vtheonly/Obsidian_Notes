# 1.7. Hierarchical Action Space: Reaction Selection and Two-Tier Synthon Retrieval

## Tier 1 — Reaction selection

\[
l_{\mathrm{rxn}}=f_{\mathrm{rxn}}(z_t)\in\mathbb R^{24}
\]

with a legal reaction mask from the handle-state tracker:

\[
P(r_t=k\mid z_t)
=
\operatorname{Softmax}(l_{\mathrm{rxn}}+M_{\mathrm{legal}})_k.
\]

## Tier 2 — Continuous target profile

\[
q_{\mathrm{target}}\in\mathbb R^{263}
\]

contains a 256-D chemical embedding target, desired exit-vector direction, desired fragment volume and an optional three-dimensional electronic descriptor.

## Retrieval

Filter the catalog by reaction/handle compatibility, then query HNSW/FAISS to obtain at most 128 candidates:

\[
\mathcal C_{\mathrm{top128}}
=
\operatorname{kNN}(q_{\mathrm{target}},\mathcal K_{\mathrm{legal}}).
\]

## Contextual re-ranking

Candidate embeddings are evaluated against the local pocket context:

\[
\ell_j=s(q_{\mathrm{target}},c_j,z_t)+M_j.
\]

STOP is an explicitly legal action and must not be decided by a molecular-weight threshold alone.

The retrieval index is non-differentiable. Stage-1 metric learning trains the continuous target space; the re-ranker learns context-sensitive preference over the retrieved set.
