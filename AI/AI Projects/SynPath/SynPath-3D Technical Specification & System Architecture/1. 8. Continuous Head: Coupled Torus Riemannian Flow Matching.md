# 1.8. Energy-Guided Coupled Torus Flow Matching

The continuous geometry state contains both periodic torsions and bounded compliant angles:

\[
\Theta_s=[\phi_s,\delta\theta_s],
\qquad
\phi_s\in\mathbb T^K,
\quad
\delta\theta_s\in\mathbb R^M.
\]

The learned joint velocity is:

\[
v_\Theta=[v_\phi,v_{\delta\theta}]\in\mathbb R^{K+M}.
\]

## Flow path

\[
\gamma_s=\phi_0+s\,\operatorname{wrap}(\phi_1-\phi_0).
\]

## Energy guidance

With the full compliant Jacobian

\[
J(\Theta)\in\mathbb R^{3N\times(K+M)},
\]

the guided joint velocity is:

\[
v_{\Theta,\mathrm{guided}}
=
v_\Theta-\eta(s)J(\Theta)^\top\nabla_xV_{\mathrm{rep}}(x(\Theta)).
\]

Both terms live in the same \(K+M\) joint space.

## Midpoint integration

Update torsions with periodic wrapping and compliant angles with bounded clamping:

\[
\phi_{n+1}=\operatorname{wrap}\left(\phi_n+\Delta s\,v_{\phi,\mathrm{mid}}\right),
\]

\[
\delta\theta_{n+1}
=
\operatorname{clip}
\left(
\delta\theta_n+\Delta s\,v_{\delta\theta,\mathrm{mid}},
-0.07,0.07
\right).
\]

Four midpoint steps are the initial fast-sampling budget and must be validated against higher-step references.

Mandatory DLS clash correction and mandatory MMFF94s generation minimization are not production stages; they remain explicit comparison baselines.
