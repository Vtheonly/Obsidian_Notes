# 1.5. Continuous Pharmacophore Density Field (CPDF) Engine

CPDF replaces a fixed five-anchor target head with a continuous spatial representation:

\[
\Phi_\theta(r\mid\mathcal P)\in[0,1]^6.
\]

Channels correspond to donor, acceptor, aromatic, hydrophobic, cationic and anionic target densities.

## Adaptive extraction

Evaluate the field over an internal cavity lattice or candidate point set and extract local maxima with NMS or equivalent clustering.

\[
\mathcal Y^*=
\{(x_m^*,R_m^*,\tau_m^*,\Delta G_m^*)\}_{m=1}^{M}.
\]

An initial computational cap \(1\le M\le12\) is a decoder budget rather than a biological invariant.

## Directional frames

A single field gradient does not uniquely determine SO(3). Directional target frames therefore require additional local geometry, field derivatives or a learned directional head.

## Training

Targets are generated from chemically typed interaction neighborhoods around experimental ligand poses. CPDF is a continuous planning signal, not proof that every local maximum is experimentally realized.
